Simple Workout Tracker is deliberately kept simple. No social feed, no challenges, no subscriptions, no clutter: just your exercises, your plans and your progress. Made for training in the gym, on machines, with cardio and with your own bodyweight. Set up your exercises once, put them into training plans, and log a whole exercise with a single tap.

COMPLETELY FREE, NO ADS
The app costs nothing, contains no ads and no in-app purchases, and there is no premium version. Every feature is available from the start.

ONE TAP PER EXERCISE
Tap "Done" and all open sets of an exercise are logged. If you change the weight between sets, log them one by one instead. Weight and reps come from your plan and your last training, so most of the time there is nothing to type.

MACHINES THE WAY THEY ARE
Each machine has a minimum weight and a weight increment, so +/- always lands on a weight the machine actually offers. Pick from 36 templates, from leg press and lat pulldown to treadmill, running, push-ups and plank, to set up your gym in one step.

CARDIO AND BODYWEIGHT
Cardio exercises log the duration and, if you like, the distance. Bodyweight exercises count reps or time, without any weights.

TRAINING PLANS
Create as many plans as you like and edit them right in the workout: add exercises, set weight, sets and reps, and put them in your order by drag and drop. Your default plan opens right away when you start the app. An optional rest timer counts down between sets.

CIRCUIT TRAINING
Circuit plans run the stations for you: rounds, station time, rest and countdown, with sounds and vibration. Every finished station is logged automatically.

QUICK LOG
Did 15 minutes on the treadmill outside of your plan? Log it with Quick log in a few taps.

HISTORY
See your progress as a chart: top weight per training day, minutes, distance or speed for cardio, reps or hold time for bodyweight. Compare several exercises in one chart, give each one its own color, filter by plan and time range, and edit or delete any logged entry.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no cloud: the app has no internet access and collects no data. Export all your data to a file at any time and import it again on a new phone.

Dark, light and warm color theme. Available in English and German. Open source on GitHub.
